How CampusFind Works

From a found wallet to a confirmed handover, here is exactly what the smart contract does.

The smart contract, not a server, decides who is allowed to do what.

Reported Claimed Returned
Rejected or cancelled claims go back to Reported
1

Report a Found Item

Describe what you found and where. The report is stored on-chain with your wallet recorded as the finder.

2

Claim With a Deposit

The owner claims it with a short proof and a small refundable deposit, which discourages false claims.

3

The Finder Decides

Only the finder can approve or reject a claim. A rejected or cancelled claim refunds the deposit in full.

4

Handover on Record

Approval marks the item Returned and refunds the deposit. Each step is a permanent, public event on the chain.
